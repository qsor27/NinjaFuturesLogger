Install the indicator manually

  1. Copy ExecutionExporter.cs (button below) to NinjaTrader's Documents\NinjaTrader 8\bin\Custom\Indicators\ folder.
  2. Open (or restart) NinjaTrader 8.
  3. Press F11 to open the NinjaScript Editor.
  4. Press F5 to compile.
  5. Add the ExecutionExporter indicator to any chart.

Final NinjaTrader steps

  1. Open (or restart) NinjaTrader 8.
  2. Press F11 to open the NinjaScript Editor.
  3. Press F5 to compile. ExecutionExporter should appear in the Indicators list.
  4. Right-click any chart → Indicators → add ExecutionExporter.
